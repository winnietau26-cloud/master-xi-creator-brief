One idea should come through: this is a beautifully presented gift that shares meaningful wishes with someone you care about—and can be carried on their bag, keys, or phone.

01 · A meaningful gift

Show the luxury gift box, opening experience, and fine product details.

02 · Cultural meaning

Explain courage, protection, prosperity, and fortunate transformation as traditional symbolism. Explain why these wishes make it a thoughtful gift.

03 · Everyday use

Show all three: bag, keys, and phone. Film each attachment and the finished look.

04 · One piece, one certificate

Each piece includes a certificate. Show it clearly and state 10 mg of 24K (999 fine) gold in the specified gold detail.

Two sides, two messages: Side A, 招财进宝, expresses a wish for wealth and prosperity. Side B, 狮来运转, is a lion-themed wish for a fortunate turn. Show both inscriptions clearly; these English explanations interpret the product-image wording.

Gold claims and the certificate concern the documented gold component or sample, not the entire pendant. Describe cultural wishes, not guaranteed outcomes; describe packaging and workmanship as seen in your actual sample.

A suggested 100–110 second flow; the final video must be at least 60 seconds. SHOOT describes what to capture. SAY gives natural talking prompts, not lines you have to read verbatim.

Prepare the pendant, gift box, certificate, everyday bag, key ring, and a phone case with a suitable attachment. Film in bright light with English narration and readable English subtitles.

Use an opening that feels natural to you. Mentioning a specific recipient is optional.

A friend’s new chapter
“My friend is starting a new chapter, and I wanted a gift that says, ‘I’m cheering you on.’”

Connect a graduation, new job, or move to the lion’s courage and protection symbolism. Close with a personal wish for their next chapter.

A milestone worth celebrating
“For someone celebrating a big milestone, I wanted a gift with a message for what comes next.”

For a promotion or new venture, connect the design’s prosperity and fortunate-transformation symbolism to your good wishes.

Someone you care about
“Sometimes the best part of a gift is the message it carries. I’d give this little lion as a way of saying, ‘I’m thinking of you.’”

For an adult family member, partner, or close friend, focus on a thoughtful gift they can keep close in daily life.

05

Delivery & the Don’ts

Do / deliver

  1. Confirm marketing usage permission before filming.
  2. Within 10 days of receiving the product, send an accessible preview link + five high-resolution lifestyle photos.
  3. Make requested revisions and send an updated preview. Wait for Master Xi’s explicit approval.
  4. Publish the approved video with the product link and accept the Collab invitation.
  5. Send the live post link and a working download link to the original, full-resolution approved video. A preview-only or social-media link is not the downloadable original file.

Don’t

  • Do not publish before approval.
  • Do not submit landscape footage inside a portrait canvas.
  • Do not hide your face during detail shots or demonstrations.
  • Do not skip either inscription, the certificate, or one of the three uses.
  • Do not describe the whole pendant as solid gold or promise financial, career, or protective results.

Product-exchange collaboration: Master Xi provides the product in exchange for the agreed content deliverables; no monetary payment is provided. Revise and resubmit content that does not meet this brief, and obtain Master Xi’s approval before publishing.
